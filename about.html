OUR STORY SINCE 2017

Rooted in our community.
Inspired by possibility.

Cears Toffees began in 2017 in Nintavur, Sri Lanka. Founder M. I. M. Ilyas wanted to create confectionery that spoke to local tastes while bringing new flavour experiences to everyday moments.

Working with his team, he experimented with textures and flavours using traditional knowledge and modern machinery. From creamy milk and cappuccino to fruity and nut-inspired treats, the collection continued to evolve.

As the business grew, so did its ambition: share a little more sweetness, reach more people, and explore opportunities beyond Sri Lanka.
